Kutsal Kitaplar - Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2, 2026

Summary

Kutsal Kitaplar does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your notes, bookmarks, reading progress, language/theme preferences, and chapters downloaded for offline reading only on your own device.

Internet access

The app connects to the internet to download scripture text from open sources (e.g. alquran.cloud, bible.helloao.org, api-docs.quran.foundation) for reading. Turkish content is bundled on-device and works fully offline.

Notifications

The daily verse reminder is entirely optional, scheduled locally on the device, and never transmits any data. You can disable it in Settings.

Children's privacy

Since the app collects no data, no data is collected from children either.
